## About

In this module, you'll explore the fundamental concepts of containerized microservices, their benefits compared to traditional monolithic architectures, and the challenges associated with adopting microservices in real-world scenarios.

## Content

| Lesson | Est. Delivery Time | Skills |
| ------ |:------------------:| ------ |
| [Concepts](./concepts/README.md)                                                                   | 10 min | Explore containerization and microservices.                  |
| [Monoliths vs. Microservices](./monoliths-vs-microservices/README.md)                              |  5 min | Compare microservices and monolithic architectures.          |
| [Benefits and Challenges of Microservices](./benefits-and-challenges-of-microservices/README.md)   |  5 min | Understand the advantages and disadvantage of microservices. |
| [A Real-World Application of Microservices](./a-real-world-application-of-microservices/README.md) | 10 min | Real-life case study on microservices usage.                 |
